Overview

An original scroll-driven WordPress experience that presents digital work through motion, WebGL and a clear project narrative while retaining practical mobile and performance fallbacks.

Motion with a reason to exist.

The experience uses a persistent navigation layer, staged content chapters and an interactive visual canvas to communicate how an idea moves from discovery to a working digital product.

The implementation is packaged as a maintainable WordPress experience rather than a one-off visual experiment, with responsive imagery, reduced-motion support, visibility pausing and performance budgets considered from the start.
