Overview

A web-based commercial management system designed around the real operations of a Greek automotive-parts business — not a static dashboard mockup.

Business logic made visible and usable.

The application connects inventory, customer and supplier records, sales, purchasing, cash movements, documents, permissions and reporting in one consistent environment.

I designed the product structure and responsive UX, built the data model and workflows, planned safe legacy-data migration and developed automated coverage for quantities, money, tax calculations, permissions and critical commercial flows.

Core product areas

01

Inventory

Multiple codes, substitutes, supplier data, vehicle applications, counts and a complete stock ledger.

02

Commercial Flows

Sales, purchases, returns, documents, payments, receipts and cash operations.

03

Relationships

Customer and supplier histories, balances, credit policies and pricing rules.

04

Granular roles, permissions, audit trails, notifications and internal collaboration.

05

Reporting

Sales, stock, receivables, aging and operational performance views.

06

Migration

Controlled import from a legacy ERP using dry runs and reconciliation checks.

A safe demo, not exposed source code.

The public demonstration is designed to use synthetic data in a dedicated read-only session. It does not expose source code, exports, administrative functions, files or external-service access.

The system is an active functional prototype. Commercial production use requires business-specific configuration, final tax-integration verification and an organized production deployment.
